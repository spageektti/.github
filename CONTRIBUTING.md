# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing!

This project is developed and maintained by a single person in their spare time. To keep maintenance sustainable and protect limited time, please review these guidelines before opening an issue or a pull request.

---

## 1. Maintainer Capacity & Scope

Because this is a solo-maintained project:

- **Quality over quantity:** Every pull request requires careful review, testing, and long-term maintenance. High-quality, focused contributions are deeply appreciated.
- **Discuss large changes first:** Before writing code for major features, significant refactors, or new dependencies, **open an issue first** to discuss whether the change aligns with the project’s roadmap. Unsolicited large PRs may be rejected to avoid wasted effort.
- **Review delays are normal:** Please be patient when waiting for a review or response. Pinging repeatedly across multiple threads is discouraged.

---

## 2. Policy on AI-Generated Content & Spam

To prevent maintainer burnout and preserve project quality, the following rules are strictly enforced:

- **No unverified AI contributions:** Pull requests or issues generated largely or entirely by large language models (LLMs) without thorough human review, understanding, and testing are strictly prohibited.
- **Hallucinated or low-effort submissions:** Contributions with hallucinated flags, non-existent APIs, inaccurate documentation, or superficial changes (e.g., mass-renaming variables with no clear benefit) will be closed immediately.
- **Off-topic content & self-promotion:** Unrelated discussions, spam, and unsolicited promotional links are not permitted.
- **Enforcement:** Low-effort AI output, spam, or bad-faith submissions **may be closed immediately without detailed feedback**. Repeat offenders or automated spam accounts will be blocked from the repository without prior warning.

If you use AI tools as an assistant (e.g., Copilot for autocompletion or sanity-checking code), that is acceptable **only if** you personally understand every line of code submitted, have tested it thoroughly, and can explain and defend your implementation.
